## About the game

Your opponent is not a bot. It is a recording of a real player: it replays their fight second by second, but shoots at you live, with the accuracy and reaction time of whoever recorded it. Use cover — walls block its shots for real.

A match runs to five round wins. Six weapons with recoil and reloading, a scoped sniper rifle, headshots deal double damage. Five arenas, each with its own time of day, from sunset to deep night.

Win and your best round is saved as a ghost. Send the code to a friend and they play against you even while you are offline. There is a map and skin editor too: build your own arena and challenge someone on it.

## Short description

Duel the ghost of a real player in a voxel shooter.

## How to play

Desktop: WASD to move, mouse to look, left button to fire, right button for the sniper scope, R to reload, Space to jump, Esc to pause. Mobile: left half of the screen is the movement stick, right half looks around, fire, jump and reload buttons sit on the right.

The pistol is always with you, the other five weapons lie on the map and respawn in ten seconds. Magazines are limited — watch the counter and reload behind cover. A match runs to five round wins and pays ghostcoins: more for a clean sweep and for a harder opponent. Spend them on skins in the shop.

## Developer comment for moderation

Language comes from the SDK i18n.lang, Russian and English are supported, and a manual switch is stored in settings. Progress and wallet go through Player.setData with a localStorage fallback for local runs. Rewarded ads are used only to double the match reward and to grant a fair rematch from zero. Interstitial ads run after a match with a 180 second cooldown. In-app purchases for Yan are disabled by a configuration flag in this version and no products are registered in the console. Challenge sharing never points to third-party domains: inside the platform it uses only the catalog page with a payload, or plain code copying. Controls work fully with mouse and touch.
